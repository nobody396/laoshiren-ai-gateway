# Version and resume compensation safely

Compensation Drafts are immutable. Any operator adjustment creates a Compensation Draft Revision that preserves the prior proposal and records the reason, actor, and before-and-after values before approval. Execution is independently idempotent for each Incident, user, and benefit channel, so a partial batch resumes only incomplete work and exact readback protects completed benefits from duplication. Incident records, draft revisions, approvals, calculation snapshots, and Compensation Evidence Snapshots are retained for at least three years; executed balance and Builder Pass ledger entries are permanent. Raw request logs may follow their normal retention policy because the evidence snapshot preserves source request identifiers, qualified facts, policy versions, exclusions, and integrity hashes needed to reproduce the decision.
