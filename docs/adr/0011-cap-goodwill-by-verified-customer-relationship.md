# Cap goodwill by verified customer relationship

The initial Shadow Compensation Policy uses a CNY 30 equivalent hourly Product Compensation Rate for every Status Product before group and tier weights. Raw duration-based value is never the final benefit by itself. For each eligible user and Incident, the system sums product- and group-specific raw values and pays the minimum of that sum, the Customer Tier cap, and ten percent of rolling 90-day Verified Paid Value. This Customer Relationship Cap prevents a short-history customer from receiving compensation disproportionate to verified payments while preserving larger recovery benefits for established customers. A Rolling Goodwill Cap adds a 30-day exposure guardrail for Standard and Priority: Standard is limited to the lower of CNY 20 equivalent or twenty percent of rolling 90-day Verified Paid Value, while Priority is limited to the lower of CNY 150 equivalent or thirty percent. Strategic has no Rolling Goodwill Cap but retains every per-Incident cap. Erroneous Charge Refunds remain exact restitution and are outside all goodwill caps.
