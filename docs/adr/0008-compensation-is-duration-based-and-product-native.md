# Compensation is duration-based and product-native

Goodwill compensation uses one duration-based formula: Compensable Impact Duration multiplied by a Product Compensation Rate, the incident-frozen Group Compensation Weight, and Customer Tier Multiplier, with failure count serving as eligibility evidence rather than a second additive payout. A user becomes eligible after at least four distinct final failed Customer Requests across the same Incident; retries, attempts, probes, excluded client or business-limit errors, and finally recovered requests do not count. Once eligible, raw value is calculated separately for each affected Status Product and User-facing Group and then summed. Compensable Impact Duration sums Customer Impact Segments after that user's first qualifying final failure for each product: each segment ends when reliable recovery evidence moves the product into Monitoring, healthy gaps and the subsequent observation period are excluded, and a Monitoring relapse adds another segment to the same Incident. Final goodwill is the minimum of raw value, the Customer Tier cap, and the Customer Relationship Cap. Pay-as-you-go customers receive equal numeric balance and Builder Pass customers receive equal numeric displayed credit from the CNY-equivalent result. If both channels are affected and a cap applies, the capped value is split between them in proportion to their uncapped raw values. Final benefits are rounded to two decimal places with no minimum and validity is not extended. Final execution waits until the incident is Resolved and explicitly approved; Erroneous Charge Refunds remain a separate exact-reversal path and are not capped as goodwill.
