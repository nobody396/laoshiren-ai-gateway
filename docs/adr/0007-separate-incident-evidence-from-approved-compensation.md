# Separate incident evidence from approved compensation

Incidents keep an Observation Window for reliability analysis and a Customer Impact Window for affected-user analysis. Every real user with a Customer-impacting Failure enters the affected set, exclusions and eligibility are evaluated transparently by a Compensation Policy, and the system may generate a Compensation Draft but never execute financial or entitlement writes without explicit approval and idempotent readback. A draft whose total exceeds ten percent of the affected Status Products' rolling 30-day Verified Paid Value becomes a High-value Compensation Draft: it cannot be approved unchanged or automatically scaled, and instead requires a redesigned replacement draft followed by explicit owner confirmation. After approved benefits execute and pass exact readback, the system sends one Compensation Notice per user and Incident; public incident updates never expose individual compensation details.
